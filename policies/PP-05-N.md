## §1 Supplier
Dairy supplier SUP-014 supplies UHT milk, lben and plain yogurt to all North stores.

## §2 Cost change
From 1 Oct 2026 the unit cost of UHT milk 1 L rises from 5.30 MAD to 5.62 MAD (+6.0%). Other SUP-014 costs are unchanged.

## §3 Promotion funding
SUP-014 funds 0% of yogurt promotions from 1 Oct to 31 Dec 2026.

## §4 Pass-through
The cost change in §2 may be passed to shelf prices within PP-04 limits, effective no earlier than 1 Oct 2026.

## §5 Review
These terms are reviewed on 31 Mar 2027.
