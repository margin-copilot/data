## §1 Purpose
This policy sets who may change shelf prices at Riad Market and how each change is recorded.

## §2 Roles
### 2.1
A regional pricing manager proposes and applies price changes for the stores in their region, within the limits in PP-04.
### 2.2
The pricing director approves changes outside those limits and every Tier A increase. The pricing director can act in all regions.

## §3 Records
Every change is logged in the price history with the proposer, the approver where one is required, a reason code and the policy clause relied on.

## §4 The pricing assistant
A proposal drafted by the pricing assistant is not a price change. It takes effect only after a person approves it in the approval screen. Approval rights follow §2.

## §5 Reason codes
COST supplier cost change · COMP competitor move · PROMO-END end of a promotion · RANGE new or delisted product · ERROR correction.

## §6 Terms
KVI: known value item, a product customers use to judge our prices. Price index: our shelf price divided by the reference competitor's shelf price, times 100. Tier: A, B or C, as defined in PP-02.
