## §1 Tier A (KVI)
Fresh milk, baguette, sugar, soft wheat flour, sunflower oil, green tea, mineral water, tomatoes, potatoes, onions.

## §2 Tier B (core range)
UHT milk, plain yogurt, lben, drinking yogurt, butter, msemen, sliced bread, couscous, lentils, tomato paste, olive oil, oranges, laundry powder.

## §3 Tier C (long tail)
Cheese spread, fresh cream, orange juice, cola, Medjool dates, dish soap, bleach.

## §4 Price index targets
Per region, against the reference competitor: Tier A between 98 and 102. Tier B between 97 and 105. Tier C has no target.

## §5 Which tier applies
The tier in the product master on the day of the change. The pricing director reviews the list every quarter.
