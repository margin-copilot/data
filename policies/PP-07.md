title: Ramadan Pricing Circular 2026
audience: all
---

## §1 Scope
All stores, from 18 Feb to 19 Mar 2026.

## §2 Tier A
No price increases on Tier A products during Ramadan, even with pricing director approval.

## §3 Dates and Tier C
Prices may rise by at most 5%, with pricing director approval.

## §4 Promotions
The promotion calendar is frozen two weeks before Ramadan starts.

## §5 Availability
Milk, flour and dates get priority for stock in all stores.
