## §1 Monthly category margin floors
Per region: Dairy 21%, Bakery 12%, Grocery 14%, Beverages 18%, Produce 10%, Household 22%.

## §2 Amber
A region's category margin is within 1 point above its floor. The pricing director reviews it within 5 working days.

## §3 Red
A region's category margin is below its floor. The regional pricing manager submits a recovery plan within 10 working days.

## §4 Confidentiality
The floors are shared only with the pricing directors group.
