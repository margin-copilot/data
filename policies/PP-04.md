title: Price Change Rules
audience: all

## §1 Scope
Permanent shelf price changes. Promotions follow PP-03.

## §2 General rules
### 2.1
Prices are set per product per region. All stores in a region carry the same shelf price.
### 2.2
A product may have at most one price increase per region in any 30-day window.
### 2.3
Every change carries a reason code from PP-01 §5.

## §3 Limits per increase
### 3.1
Tier A: every increase needs pricing director approval, may not exceed 3%, and must keep the price index at or below 102.
### 3.2
Tier B: the regional pricing manager may apply increases of up to 4%. Larger increases need pricing director approval and may never exceed 10%.
### 3.3
Tier C: the regional pricing manager may apply increases of up to 8%. Larger increases need pricing director approval.
### 3.4
Fresh produce (tomatoes, potatoes, onions, oranges) may be repriced weekly to follow market cost. §2.2 and §3.1–3.3 do not apply, but the price index must stay at or below 105.
### 3.5
The regional pricing manager may lower a price at any time, unless the cut takes the category below its margin floor. That needs pricing director approval.

## §4 Competitor moves
When the reference competitor lowers a Tier A price, the regional pricing manager may match it within 48 hours with reason code COMP and no further approval.

## §5 Cost pass-through
Supplier cost increases may be passed to shelf prices within the limits of §3. Regional supplier terms in PP-05 apply on top.
