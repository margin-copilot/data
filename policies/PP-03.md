## §1 Scope
Temporary price reductions in all stores. Permanent changes follow PP-04.

## §2 Depth limits
### 2.1
Tier A: at most 15% off, for at most 14 days.
### 2.2
Tier B: at most 30% off.
### 2.3
Any promotion deeper than 20% needs at least 50% supplier funding, recorded on the promotion before it starts.
### 2.4
Tier C: at most 40% off.

## §3 Margin
If a promotion is forecast to take a category below its regional margin floor for the month, the pricing director must approve it first.

## §4 Overlap
One active promotion per product per region at a time.

## §5 Ending a promotion
Ending a promotion returns the shelf price to its pre-promotion level. This is not a price increase under PP-04 and uses reason code PROMO-END.

## §6 Ramadan
The promotion calendar is frozen two weeks before Ramadan starts. See PP-07.
